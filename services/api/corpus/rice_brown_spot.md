# Brown Spot Disease in Rice (Bipolaris oryzae)

## Symptoms

Brown spot is a fungal disease caused by *Bipolaris oryzae* (formerly *Helminthosporium oryzae*). It affects leaves, sheaths, and grains.

**Key indicators:**
- Small, oval to circular brown spots on leaves with grey or whitish centres
- Lesions surrounded by a yellow halo
- Spots may coalesce, causing large necrotic areas
- Grain discoloration (dark brown or black patches on husk)

## Conditions Favouring the Disease

Brown spot is strongly associated with **nutrient-deficient soils**, particularly low silicon and potassium. It is prevalent in rainfed lowland systems, poorly drained fields, and drought-stressed crops. Warm temperatures (25–30°C) with high humidity promote spore germination.

## Management

### Cultural Controls
1. Apply balanced fertilization — ensure adequate **potassium** (K₂O at 40 kg/ha)
2. Improve field drainage to avoid waterlogging
3. Use certified disease-free seed treated with fungicide

### Chemical Controls
1. Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5 g/L as foliar spray
2. Propiconazole 25% EC at 1 mL/L for moderate to severe cases
3. Seed treatment with Carbendazim 50% WP at 2 g/kg seed

### When to Escalate
Escalate if brown spot is seen on more than 25% of leaves AND grain filling has started — yield loss risk is high at this stage.
