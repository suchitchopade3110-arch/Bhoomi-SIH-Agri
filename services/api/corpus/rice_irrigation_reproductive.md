# Water Management for Rice at Reproductive Stage

## Critical Water Period

The reproductive stage (panicle initiation through flowering, typically days 45–80 after transplanting) is the most water-sensitive period in the rice crop cycle. Water stress during this stage can cause:

- Spikelet sterility (empty grains)
- Reduced panicle size
- Delayed heading
- Yield loss of 30–50%

## Standing Water Depth

Maintain 5–7 cm of standing water continuously from panicle initiation through flowering. Do NOT practice alternate wetting and drying (AWD) during this stage — continuous flooding is critical.

## Irrigation Frequency

- Irrigate every 2–3 days to maintain standing water
- Never allow the field to dry out during flowering
- Monitor weather forecasts — supplement irrigation ahead of hot, dry spells

## Grain Filling Phase

After flowering, gradually reduce water:
- 5 cm standing water during early grain filling (days 80–95)
- Allow intermittent drying during late grain filling (days 95–110)
- Stop irrigation 10–15 days before harvest to promote uniform ripening

## Temperature Considerations

High temperatures (above 35°C) during flowering cause spikelet sterility. Maintain deeper water (7–10 cm) during heat waves to cool the root zone and buffer temperature.
