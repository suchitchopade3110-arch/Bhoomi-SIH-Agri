# Seed Selection, Seed Rate, and Nursery Management for Rice

## Variety Selection (Samba Paddy Focus)

For Tamil Nadu samba season (June–July sowing, October–January harvest):
- **ADT 36**: 135 days, fine grain, BLB tolerant, good for irrigated lowlands
- **ADT 39**: 140 days, medium-slender grain, blast resistant
- **BPT 5204 (Samba Mahsuri)**: 145 days, fine grain, very popular for cooking quality
- **CO 51**: 110 days, short duration, blast and BLB moderately resistant
- **Improved Samba Mahsuri (ISM)**: 140 days, bacterial blight resistant line

## Seed Rate

- **Transplanted paddy**: 40–60 kg/ha (16–24 kg/acre)
- **Direct seeded rice (DSR)**: 80–100 kg/ha (32–40 kg/acre)
- **System of Rice Intensification (SRI)**: 5–7.5 kg/ha (2–3 kg/acre)

## Seed Treatment

1. **Salt water selection**: Dissolve 1.5 kg common salt in 10 L water. Immerse seeds — remove floaters (chaffy/damaged), retain sinkers.
2. **Fungicide treatment**: Treat with Carbendazim 50% WP at 2 g/kg seed OR Pseudomonas fluorescens at 10 g/kg seed.
3. **Soaking**: Soak treated seeds in water for 24 hours, then incubate in a gunny bag for 24 hours until sprouted.

## Nursery Management

### Wet Nursery
- **Area**: 1/20th of main field area (e.g., 400 m² nursery for 1 acre)
- **Bed preparation**: Puddled, levelled beds 1.5 m wide, 15 cm raised, with 30 cm channels
- **Fertilizer**: Apply 100 g DAP per m² bed area, incorporate before sowing
- **Sowing density**: 40–50 g sprouted seed per m² of nursery bed

### Nursery Care
- Maintain thin film of water (1–2 cm) after seedling emergence
- Apply N top-dress at 15 DAS if seedlings are pale
- Pull seedlings at 20–25 days for transplanting (SRI: 12–14 days)
- Handle seedlings gently — avoid root damage during pulling
