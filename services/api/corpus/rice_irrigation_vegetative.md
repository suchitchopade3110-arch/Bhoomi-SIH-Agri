# Water Management for Rice at Vegetative Stage

## Standing Water Requirements

During the vegetative stage (tillering phase, typically days 15–45 after transplanting), rice requires standing water at a depth of 2–5 cm. Maintaining this thin layer of standing water is critical for:

- Suppressing weed growth
- Maintaining soil temperature stability
- Ensuring adequate nutrient uptake through the root zone

## Irrigation Scheduling

**Recommended schedule:**
- Irrigate every 3–5 days to maintain 2–5 cm standing water
- Allow fields to dry for 1–2 days between irrigations (alternate wetting and drying — AWD) to promote root growth
- Monitor soil moisture: irrigate when surface water recedes and soil starts to crack

**ET-based scheduling:**
- Reference evapotranspiration (ET₀) for Tamil Nadu during samba season: 4.0–5.5 mm/day
- Crop coefficient (Kc) at vegetative stage: 1.05–1.15
- Crop water requirement (ETc) = ET₀ × Kc ≈ 4.5–6.0 mm/day
- Add 3–5 mm/day for percolation losses in clay loam soils

## Signs of Water Stress

- **Over-watering**: Yellowing of lower leaves, algal growth on water surface, root rot symptoms
- **Under-watering**: Leaf rolling (especially midday), stunted tillers, delayed growth stage

## Daily Water Budget

For a 2-acre samba paddy field on clay loam soil:
- ETc: ~5 mm/day → 40,500 litres/day (2 acres = 8,094 m², 5 mm × 8,094 = 40,470 L)
- Percolation: ~4 mm/day → 32,376 L/day
- Total: ~72,000–75,000 litres/day during peak vegetative stage

## Drainage

Drain fields 7–10 days before top-dressing nitrogen to allow soil aeration. Re-flood 2–3 days after fertilizer application.
