# When and How to Harvest Paddy

## Visual Indicators of Maturity

The optimal harvest time for paddy rice is when:
- 80–85% of grains in the panicle have turned golden-yellow
- The straw (stem and leaves) has turned from green to straw-yellow
- Grains are firm and resist denting when pressed with a fingernail
- The panicle droops under the weight of mature grains

## Grain Moisture Content

- **Optimal harvest moisture**: 20–24% grain moisture content
- **Too early** (above 25%): high drying costs, grain cracking during drying
- **Too late** (below 18%): grain shattering losses, lodging risk, pest damage
- Use a moisture meter if available; otherwise rely on the fingernail dent test

## Timing by Variety

| Variety | Duration (days after sowing) | Season |

| Ponni (BPT 5204) | 130–145 | Oct–Jan |
| CO 51 | 110–115 | Kuruvai/Navarai |
| ADT 43 | 120–125 | Samba/Navarai |
| IR 20 | 120–130 | All seasons |

## Harvest Method

1. **Manual**: Cut at 15–20 cm above ground level using sickle. Bundle and stack for 2–3 days before threshing.
2. **Combine harvester**: Adjust header height to minimize stubble, set threshing cylinder speed to avoid grain cracking.

## Post-Harvest

Dry harvested paddy to 12–14% moisture for safe storage. Sun-drying on a clean surface in thin layers (5–7 cm) for 2–3 days is standard. Avoid drying on roads due to contamination.
