# Nitrogen Application Schedule for Paddy Rice

## Recommended Nitrogen Rate

For samba paddy on clay loam soil (Tamil Nadu conditions):
- **Total N**: 100–120 kg/ha (or 40–50 kg/acre)
- Split into 3 applications for maximum efficiency

## Timing of Application

### Split 1: Basal (at transplanting)
- 40% of total N (40–48 kg/ha)
- Apply as DAP (18-46-0) or urea (46-0-0) and incorporate into puddled soil
- Drain the field before application, then flood after 2–3 days

### Split 2: Active Tillering (20–25 days after transplanting)
- 30% of total N (30–36 kg/ha)
- Apply as urea top-dress on standing water (2–3 cm depth)
- Do not apply if heavy rain is expected within 24 hours (N loss via runoff)

### Split 3: Panicle Initiation (45–50 days after transplanting)
- 30% of total N (30–36 kg/ha)
- Critical for grain yield — this split determines panicle size and grain number
- Use leaf colour chart (LCC) to confirm N need: apply only if LCC reading is below 4

## Dangers of Excess Nitrogen

- **Increases BLB risk**: Succulent, nitrogen-rich tissue is highly susceptible to *Xanthomonas oryzae*
- **Promotes lodging**: Tall, weak stems that fall over, especially during wind/rain
- **Delays maturity**: Extended vegetative growth at the expense of grain filling
- **Increases pest attraction**: Leafhoppers and stem borers prefer nitrogen-rich plants

## Urea vs DAP

| Fertilizer | N-P-K | When to Use |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| Urea (46-0-0) | 46% N | Top-dress during active growth |
| DAP (18-46-0) | 18% N, 46% P | Basal application (provides phosphorus) |
| MOP (0-0-60) | 60% K | Basal + panicle initiation |

## Note on Organic Nitrogen

Green manure (Sesbania or Daincha) incorporated before transplanting can supply 25–30 kg N/ha, reducing the urea requirement proportionally.
