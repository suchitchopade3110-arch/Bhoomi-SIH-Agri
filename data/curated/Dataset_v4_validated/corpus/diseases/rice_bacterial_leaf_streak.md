# TNAU & ICAR Rice Disease Management Guide: Bacterial Leaf Streak (BLS)

## 1. Overview & Crop Stages Affected
Bacterial Leaf Streak (BLS), caused by *Xanthomonas oryzae* pv. *oryzicola*, enters the leaf blade through stomata and mechanical wounds. Unlike BLB, which attacks the vascular system, BLS infects the parenchymatous mesophyll tissue between veins.

## 2. Distinguishing Field Identification Cues
- Fine, narrow, water-soaked, translucent interveinal streaks confined between leaf veins (unlike the wavy marginal lesions of BLB).
- Tiny yellow bead-like bacterial exudates dry into amber crusts along the streaks in sunlight.
- Under transmitted light, the streaks appear bright translucent yellow.
- As lesions age, they turn yellowish-brown to dark brown and coalesce into extensive necrotic patches.

## 3. Integrated Disease Management
- **Avoid Mechanical Injury**: Avoid clipping seedling tips during transplanting.
- **Bactericidal Interventions (Validated)**:
  - *Copper Hydroxide 77 WP* @ 1.25 kg/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Copper Oxychloride 50 WP* @ 1.25 kg/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
