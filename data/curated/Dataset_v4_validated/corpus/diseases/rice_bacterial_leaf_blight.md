# TNAU & ICAR Rice Disease Management Guide: Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB)

## 1. Overview & Crop Stages Affected
Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB), caused by *Xanthomonas oryzae* pv. *oryzae*, is one of the most destructive vascular bacterial diseases of rice across Tamil Nadu, especially in the Cauvery Delta.

The disease manifests in two distinct phases:
1. **Kresek Phase (Seedling/Early Tillering)**: Acute systemic wilt occurring 1 to 3 weeks after transplanting, causing entire tillers to wither and die, often confused with stem borer dead hearts.
2. **Leaf Blight Phase (Tillering to Heading)**: Characterized by elongated lesions starting from leaf tips and margins with distinct wavy margins.

## 2. Distinguishing Field Identification Cues
- Water-soaked to yellowish-green translucent stripes starting at the leaf tips and margins.
- Characteristic wavy, undulated margin separating diseased yellow tissue from healthy green blade.
- Presence of minute amber-colored milky bacterial ooze droplets on lower leaf surfaces during humid mornings.
- Squeezing infected stem cut ends in clean water reveals cloudy bacterial streaming (*ooze test*).

## 3. Environmental Epidemiology & Disease Progression
- Favored by high relative humidity (>85%), temperatures between 25°C to 34°C, and heavy rainfall with strong winds.
- Excessive application of nitrogenous fertilizers significantly exacerbates disease severity.
- Deep standing water and continuous flooding accelerate field-to-field bacterial spread.

## 4. Integrated Disease Management
- **Varietal Resistance**: Cultivate resistant/tolerant varieties such as CO 51, ADT 43, and TNAU Rice TRY 3.
- **Nutrient Management**: Split nitrogen application (25% basal, 50% tillering, 25% panicle initiation); avoid late top-dressing of urea. Apply extra potassium (potash) @ 25 kg/ha to enhance plant cell wall resistance.
- **Bactericidal Interventions**:
  - *Copper Hydroxide 77 WP* @ 1.25 kg/ha in 500 L water (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Fresh Cow Dung Extract (20%)*: Spray 20% cow dung extract twice at 15-day intervals (traditional bio-control recommendation in TNAU CPG).
  - *Streptocycline (100 g/ha) + Copper Oxychloride (500 g/ha)* (`RESTRICTED` — subject to AMR regulatory review).
