# TNAU & ICAR Rice Disease Management Guide: False Smut

## 1. Overview & Crop Stages Affected
False smut, caused by *Ustilaginoidea virens*, transforms individual grains in a panicle into large velvety spore balls. It occurs during flowering and grain maturation.

High relative humidity (>90%) and rainfall during the flowering stage favor severe infection.

## 2. Distinguishing Field Identification Cues
- Individual grains on the panicle transformed into large, spherical, velvety smut balls (up to 1 cm or more in diameter).
- Color transition: Immature smut balls are yellow to orange, enclosed in a membrane; mature balls turn dark olive green to blackish-green and burst, releasing powdery chlamydospores.
- Only a few spikelets per panicle are typically affected, but the mycotoxin (*ustiloxin*) contaminates the entire harvest.

## 3. Integrated Disease Management
- **Timing of Management**: Chemical intervention is ONLY effective if applied prior to panicle emergence (booting stage). Once smut balls are visible, fungicide sprays cannot reverse the condition.
- **Fungicidal Interventions (Validated)**:
  - *Propiconazole 25 EC* @ 500 ml/ha at 50% boot leaf stage (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Copper Hydroxide 77 WP* @ 1.25 kg/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
