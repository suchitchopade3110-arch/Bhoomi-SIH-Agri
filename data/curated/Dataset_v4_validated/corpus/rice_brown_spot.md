# TNAU & ICAR Rice Disease Management Guide: Brown Spot

## 1. Overview & Crop Stages Affected
Brown spot, caused by *Bipolaris oryzae* (*Helminthosporium oryzae*), is a seed-borne and airborne fungal disease strongly associated with poor soil fertility, zinc/potassium deficiencies, and moisture stress. It occurs from nursery to mature grain stages.

## 2. Distinguishing Field Identification Cues
- Oval, circular, or sesame seed-shaped dark brown spots on leaf blades, sheaths, and glumes.
- Mature spots possess a light brown to grey center surrounded by a reddish-brown margin and bright yellow halo.
- Infected glumes develop dark brown to black discolored patches, leading to seedling blight during germination and high grain chaffiness.

## 3. Integrated Disease Management
- **Soil Fertility Correction**: Apply balanced NPK along with zinc sulphate @ 25 kg/ha. Correct potassium deficiency.
- **Seed Treatment**: Treat seeds with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/kg or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed.
- **Fungicidal Interventions (Validated)**:
  - *Mancozeb 75 WP* @ 1000 g/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Carbendazim + Mancozeb 75 WP* @ 750 g/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
