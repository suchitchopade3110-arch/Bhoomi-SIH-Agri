# TNAU & IRRI Rice Disease Management Guide: Sheath Blight

## 1. Overview & Crop Stages Affected
Sheath Blight, caused by the soil-borne sclerotial fungus *Rhizoctonia solani*, is a major fungal disease of rice in high-density, heavily fertilized plantings. It typically initiates during maximum tillering and advances rapidly through the panicle emergence stage.

The sclerotia survive in soil and floating crop debris, floating onto emerging leaf sheaths during initial flooding and penetrating epidermal cells.

## 2. Distinguishing Field Identification Cues
- Initial lesions appear on lower leaf sheaths near the waterline as oval, oblong, or elliptical greenish-grey water-soaked spots (1–3 cm long).
- As lesions mature, they develop irregular dark reddish-brown borders surrounding a bleached greyish-white center ("snake skin" pattern).
- Small spherical white mustard seed-like mustard sclerotia form on lesion surfaces, turning dark brown as they mature.
- Lesions climb upward from lower sheaths to upper leaves, eventually enveloping the flag leaf and panicle.

## 3. Environmental Epidemiology & Disease Progression
- Favored by high relative humidity (95%–100%), warm temperatures (28°C–32°C), high plant canopy density, and excess nitrogen fertilizer.
- Spread vertically along tillers via mycelial growth and horizontally between adjacent hills by direct leaf contact.

## 4. Integrated Disease Management
- **Cultural Practices**: Avoid over-dense planting (maintain $20\text{ cm} \times 10\text{ cm}$ or $20\text{ cm} \times 15\text{ cm}$ spacing). Drain field water during tillering (alternate wetting and drying) to reduce canopy humidity.
- **Biocontrol**: Apply *Trichoderma viride* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed with 50 kg FYM at transplanting.
- **Fungicidal Interventions (Validated)**:
  - *Hexaconazole 5 SC* @ 1000 ml/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Validamycin 3 L* @ 1000 ml/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole* @ 500 ml/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
