# TNAU & ICAR Rice Disease Management Guide: Rice Blast

## 1. Overview & Crop Stages Affected
Rice Blast, caused by the ascomycete fungus *Magnaporthe oryzae* (*Pyricularia oryzae*), is an airborne fungal disease attacking rice at all developmental phases.

Depending on the plant organ infected, the disease is termed:
1. **Leaf Blast**: Most prevalent from nursery through tillering.
2. **Node Blast**: Attack on stem nodes turning them black and brittle.
3. **Neck Blast (Rotten Neck)**: Attack on the panicle base/rachis during emergence, causing the panicle to snap and drop.

## 2. Distinguishing Field Identification Cues
- Characteristic spindle-shaped (eye-shaped) lesions with pointed ends on leaf blades.
- Lesion center is typically greyish-white or ash-colored, surrounded by a dark brown or reddish-brown necrotic margin and yellow halo.
- In severe attacks, numerous lesions coalesce, causing entire leaves to dry and wither (blast-scorched canopy).
- Neck infection appears as a dark brown to blackish lesion girdling the panicle neck; panicle breaks at neck joint and remains unfilled/whitish.

## 3. Environmental Epidemiology & Disease Progression
- Favored by cool night temperatures (18°C–24°C) coupled with high daytime relative humidity (>90%) and long dew periods (>=10 hours).
- High nitrogen application creates tender succulent leaves highly susceptible to appressorium penetration.

## 4. Integrated Disease Management
- **Varietal Resistance**: Grow blast-resistant or tolerant cultivars suited to the season (e.g., ADT 45, TPS 5).
- **Seed Treatment**: Treat seeds with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed.
- **Nutrient Regulation**: Avoid high single-dose nitrogen applications. Apply silica/potash to increase epidermal leaf hardness.
- **Fungicidal Interventions (Validated)**:
  - *Tricyclazole 75 WP* @ 500 g/ha (0.6 g/L water) at early tillering and panicle emergence (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Isoprothiolane 40 EC* @ 750 ml/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
  - *Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC* @ 500 ml/ha (`VERIFIED_CURRENT`).
